## 2. What the Game Is

**Stated by kean (20 Nov 2026):**

This is a game about **roughness** — mischief, destruction, and revenge filtered through cartoon joy. The cultural spine is Indonesian folklore/practice around ***tumbal proyek***: a sacrificial offering made so a building or project stays safe and succeeds. Colleagues sacrifice a construction worker into the theme-park project. After the park is built, that worker rises as a young mischievous evil spirit, adamant about destroying the park and getting revenge — and while doing so, **finds joy in creating chaos**.

Tone: **whimsical and comedic**, but **a bit morbid**. Humor is from Ruckus’s perspective (e.g. electrocuting a crowd in a puddle is funny for him; it is still lethal for the people in the puddle).

**Carried from prior locked pillars (not restated by kean today; still assumed unless overturned):**

1. Whimsical art style  
2. Dark, powerful theme  
3. Blurred lines (moral ambiguity — comedy vs harm)

**Working product framing (prior context, still useful):** darkly whimsical possession-puzzle / sandbox simulation where the spirit uses park workers and guests to set up slapstick disaster chains. Park brand in build/art: **WoohooLand**.

## 4. Player Flow (High Level)

```
Float as spirit → observe park → possess the right person → use their access/tools
→ set up a prank chain → enjoy morbid slapstick payoff → next puzzle / escalate the ride
```

1. **Observe** who has what (keys, booth access, tools, items).
2. **Possess** by getting close to a person (build: touch-range spirit possession).
3. **Act** only as far as that host’s job/items allow.
4. **Chain** small actions into a bigger disaster (flood a puddle → live cable → lure mechanic → cut power → …).
5. **Payoff** reads as comedy from Ruckus’s POV, even when the outcome is deadly/cartoon-violent.

## 6. Puzzle Design Intent

- Puzzles are **whimsical and comedic**, and **a bit morbid**.
- Solutions are **possession + right person + right object**, not abstract inventory menus alone.
- Example (Puzzle 1): flooding + electricity is funny *because* we play as Ruckus; the fiction still kills everyone in the puddle.
- Chains should feel like planned chaos: small setup → loud payoff → next gated role becomes available.
