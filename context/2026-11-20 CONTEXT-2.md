## 2. What the Game Is

**WoohooLand** is a game about **Ruckus**. He was a construction worker for the theme park. His colleagues sacrificed him. The team is inspired by Indonesian folklore/practice ***Tumbal Proyek***: a sacrificial offering so a building or project stays safe and succeeds. Ruckus was that sacrifice.

After the park is built, he rises as a **young mischievous “evil” spirit** — “evil” here means **funny and whimsical**, not grim real-world evil. He wants to destroy the park and get revenge, and he **finds joy in chaos and destruction**.

### Design pillars (still in force)

1. Whimsical art style  
2. Dark, powerful theme  
3. Blurred lines (comedy vs harm — harm reads as cartoon, not gore)

### Tone & content limits (confirmed)

- Lead with **softer, funnier cartoon** outcomes. Example: trampled by the Ferris Wheel → show a **squished cartoon character**, not realistic injury.
- **No blood.**  
- **No kids** as victims / targets.  
- No grim evil (rape, torture, etc.). Mischief stays **whimsical**.

Humor is from Ruckus’s point of view; outcomes stay cartoon-safe.

  - **Mechanic** → keys → **electrical box**

  - **Operator** → **control panel**

- **No possession timer**

## 5. Player Flow

```
Spirit → observe who has what → possess the right person → use their tools/access
→ set up a cartoon disaster chain → funny payoff → next puzzle
```

1. Observe roles and objects.  
2. Possess the host who can do the next gated action.  
3. Interact with items as that host.  
4. Chain setups into slapstick (e.g. big puddle → electricity → lure mechanic → cut power).  
5. Payoff is **cartoon-funny**, not grim.
